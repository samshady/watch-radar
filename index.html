Care, Storage & Toolkit Manual

Tested guidance for maintaining budget automatics, sizing bracelets for small wrists without scratching links, and protecting movements from everyday hazards.

Caliber Safety Part I

Movement Care & Safety Protocol

  • The Date Change Danger Zone: Never quickset the date between 21:00 (9 PM) and 03:00 (3 AM) on Seiko NH35 or PT5000 calibers. The date-change gears are actively engaged. Manually advance hands past midnight first.
  • Demagnetization in 5 Seconds: If an automatic suddenly runs 45 seconds fast per day, laptop speakers or iPad smart covers magnetized the hairspring. Test with a compass app, then clear it with an eight euro blue coil demagnetizer.
  • Water Resistance Reality: 50m means light surface swimming only. For swimming confidence, choose 100m or 200m watches with a screw-down crown like the Steeldive SD1970.
Rule of thumb: Hand-wind 15 turns to start, never shake violently.
Storage Part II

Small-Wrist Watch Boxes & Rolls

  • The Stiff Cushion Trap: Standard Chinese watch box pillows measure 18 to 20 cm in circumference. Forcing a bracelet sized for a 16 cm wrist over them stretches clasp springs.
  • Slide-Rail M-Cushions: Look for travel rolls with hexagonal snap rails or M-shaped compressible pillows that compress down to 14.5 cm without strain.
  • Moisture Protection: Keep one dry silica gel packet inside your 3-watch roll. Avoid storing mechanical watches in high-humidity bathrooms.
Recommended: Hexagonal 3-slot travel roll with slide-in dividers.
Customization Part III

Strap Swapping Masterclass

  • FKM Waffle & Tropic Rubber: Standard silicone collects pocket lint. Fluoroelastomer (FKM) rubber is lint-free, chemical resistant, and curves naturally around smaller wrists.
  • Marine Nationale Parachute Webbing: Elastic French parachute straps have zero pin holes. They adjust by millimeter increments and sit flat without stiff buckle gaps.
  • Watch Lug Widths: 20mm is the universal sweet spot. Watch out for 19mm odd lug widths (like the Escapement Time 35mm) which require dedicated 19mm straps.
Quick-release spring bars save your lugs from scratches.
Workbench Part IV

Essential Mod & Sizing Kit

  • Pin Punch Block & Brass Hammer: Do not use cheap plastic twist pushers on stubborn split pins. A slotted plastic holder and brass hammer give clean, controlled link removal.
  • Hollow-Ground Screwdrivers: For San Martin or Pagani screwed links, use 1.2mm or 1.4mm hollow-ground blades. Wedge tips will cam out and mar screw slots.
  • Diamond Selector II Tester: A ten euro thermal conductivity gem tester confirms genuine sapphire crystal versus mineral glass in two seconds.
Silicon 7000 grease keeps caseback O-rings pliable and waterproof.
